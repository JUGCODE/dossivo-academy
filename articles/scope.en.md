# What evidence can DOSSIVO help prepare?

DOSSIVO is a workspace for preparing evidence and sharing it under authorisation. Its full workflow focuses on FLR. CBAM, CE, REACH, EUDR and DPP are fictional-data demonstrations. Certification and official decisions belong to competent bodies.
